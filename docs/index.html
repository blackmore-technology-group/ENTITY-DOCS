BTDU proof results

Storage efficiency and destruction recovery were tested as engineering properties.

97.14% lower in the reproduced Level-3 benchmark

The fresh 2026-10-01 qualification generated 250 million bonds for 50 million entities: 4.4 GB of compact bond-plus-index state versus a 153.6 GB 768-dimension float32 vector baseline, a 97.1354167% reduction. All 1,000 sampled lookups were correct and the regenerated bond/index hashes matched the historical qualification artifacts.

100% exact qualified recovery

The fresh 2026-10-01 Memnox cold-recovery rerun rebuilt the archive, manifest and acceptance receipt from sealed ADAM/BTDU state, then verified all 865/865 Git blobs with zero missing, extra or mismatched paths. Git, GitHub, network and the source clone were not used.

Why it matters: BTDU is not only a provenance index. It is designed as a compact governed information universe whose exact state and lineage can survive loss of the original working environment when the qualified recovery conditions are met.

Economic lifecycle

From governed information to economic consequence.

DCO → INSTRUMENT → LISTING → DISCLOSURE → ORDER / RFQ / AUCTION → PRICE DISCOVERY → TRADE → CLEARING → SETTLEMENT → ENTITLEMENT → USAGE → DERIVED OUTPUT → ECONOMIC CONSEQUENCE

Rights, not artificial byte scarcity

Economic scarcity can exist in access, entitlement, capacity, duration, jurisdiction, quantity, derivation, participation and transferability while the underlying information remains non-rival.

Participation without a token

Treasury reserves, primary participation, secondary terms, derivative participation and service revenue use explicitly established terms. ENTITY imposes no automatic protocol tax.

Settlement remains evidence-bound

A trade is not automatically external payment. Authorized settlement verifiers and payment attestations preserve that boundary instead of converting execution into fictional cash settlement.

Information, state and intelligence

ENTITY, BTDU, ADAM and NIKI have separate jobs.

ENTITY

Authority and economics. Identity, authority, rights, evidence, value, market and recovery relationships.

BTDU

Governed information universe. Exact objects, atoms, bonds, compounds, lineage, indexes, repository manifests and economic topology under ENTITY authorization.

ADAM

Deterministic state engine. Applies and replays governed atomic transitions without independently inventing rights or authority.

NIKI

Bounded causal intelligence. Reasons over approved models and governed evidence without minting ownership, permission or settlement.
