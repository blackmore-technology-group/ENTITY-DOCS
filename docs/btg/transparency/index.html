What evidence BTG will and will not publish

Transparency register

Public information edition 0.1 · 20 October 2026 · Blackmore Technology Group Limited
Document status: Public explanatory draft. Engineering and legal status must be independently verified before relying on any operating or investment claim. No securities are offered here.

A transparency feed without exposing private data

BTG intends to publish non-sensitive evidence that lets readers distinguish verified events from assertions. A public report can document the existence and timestamp of an authorized activity without posting the transaction's private details.

Proposed public fields

Publication ID; time period; evidence type; document version; cryptographic commitment where privacy-reviewed; independent verifier if any; asset category; provenance status; permission class; qualification scope; limitations; withdrawal or correction notices; and whether an item reflects a real transaction or a simulation.

Never publish

User activity histories, private conversation recordings, client identities, actual keys, access tokens, exploit-level security details, private prices without permission, confidential legal documents, bank details or unlicensed datasets.

Evidence confidence

Designed is different from implemented. Internal tests are different from independent verification. A signed record is different from a lawful ownership determination. A commercial invoice is different from reconciled bank collection. A regulated market authorization must be identifiable to the specific entity and activity.

October 2026 snapshot

This portal's documents are public-information drafts. A comprehensive, independently accepted audit of all transformations and real-money marketplace readiness has not been established here. No live market ticker, order book, certified income figure or public chain-of-title assertion is published as a verified fact.

A machine-readable feed can be added after publication controls, privacy assessment and independently verified evidence exports are complete. Until then, blank or withheld fields should remain explicitly unavailable rather than populated with synthetic production-looking data.
