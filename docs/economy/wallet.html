ENTITY Economic Wallet

The ENTITY Economic Wallet makes the existing rights economy usable as a portfolio. It presents EEP positions, instruments, entitlements, orders, usage, economic obligations and treasury reserves in a stock-style interface while keeping canonical market state in the existing ENTITY Exchange Protocol and Economic Participation stores.

The wallet is not a second ledger. Rights quantities come from EEP balances. Last price comes only from settled trades. Orders provide bid/ask quotes. Entitlements, usage and obligations remain in their canonical records.

Two wallet roles

Participant Wallet

Bound to an ENTITY identity. Shows rights positions, quantity, entitlements, usage, orders/offers, cost basis, realized/unrealized change, receivables, payables and market observations.

Treasury Wallet

Bound to a distinct treasury ENTITY. Adds treasury reserve positions, participation policies, primary allocations, secondary royalties, derivative participation and economic-event visibility.

Stock-style presentation

INSTRUMENT → QUANTITY → COST BASIS → LAST / BID / ASK → INDICATIVE MARKET VALUE → REALIZED / UNREALIZED CHANGE → ORDERS → ACTIVITY

"Stock-style" describes the market and portfolio user experience. ENTITY does not infer that a DCO right, licence or entitlement is a corporate share, security, commodity, derivative or any other legal classification.

Market-value integrity

FieldENTITY wallet rule
LastOnly the latest qualifying settled trade may populate the last-price field.
BidHighest qualifying open buy order. A quote, not realized value.
AskLowest qualifying open sell order. A quote, not realized value.
Indicative market valuePosition quantity × observed last settled trade, when such a trade exists.
Unpriced positionRemains unpriced. An issuer cannot manufacture value merely by publishing an ask.
Accounting valueNot inferred. Indicative marks are not accounting fair value.

Fiat stays outside the wallet

The ENTITY wallet does not need to custody CAD, USD or another fiat currency. Money can remain at a bank or payment provider while ENTITY records the rights instrument, trade, obligation, settlement reference and verification evidence.

TRADE → OBLIGATION → EXTERNAL PAYMENT → SETTLEMENT EVIDENCE → VERIFIED SETTLED STATE
  • wallet_does_not_custody_fiat = true
  • cryptocurrency_required = false
  • protocol_tax_bps = 0
  • payment credentials are not wallet state

Wallet and DCO economy

A DCO can remain one underlying governed object while multiple EEP instruments express different bounded rights against it. The wallet then shows those rights as separate positions.

DCO → INFERENCE RIGHTS / TRAINING RIGHTS / DERIVATIVE RIGHTS / REDISTRIBUTION RIGHTS / REGIONAL EXCLUSIVES → WALLET POSITIONS → TRADING → USAGE → ECONOMIC CONSEQUENCE
