DCO Factory

The DCO Factory is the operating layer for creating thousands of economically distinct, provenance-backed DCOs without manually designing every contract. It standardizes commercial profiles, scarcity rules, duplicate/version checks and one Master Economic Record per DCO while leaving canonical identity, rights, exchange, treasury and wallet state in the existing ENTITY runtime.

First full-scale design target: 10,000 qualified DCOs and roughly 50,000–80,000 physical economic instruments, with balances, entitlements, trades and usage created only as real activity occurs.

Three standardized commercial profiles

Standard DCO

3–5 product archetypes for smaller algorithms, datasets and software components.

Advanced DCO

6–8 product archetypes for commercial assets that need distinct training, compute, enterprise, derivative or OEM rights.

Strategic DCO

9–12 product archetypes for major AI, robotics and high-value information assets.

Factory flow

BUILD ASSET → QUALIFY → PROVENANCE → DCO → RIGHTS PASSPORT → GLOBAL PASSPORT → PROFILE / BTDU → INSTRUMENTS → OPTIONAL SHARED BRIDGE → PUBLIC-SAFE EVIDENCE

The factory now enforces this order through its production-pipeline registry. Economic issuance is rejected until passport and profile/BTDU stages are complete. Production pipeline details →

Master Economic Record

Every DCO receives one management record covering identity, family, source hash, provenance, version, economic profile, instrument set, supply, treasury reserves, market state, economic state and lifecycle. It is an application index over canonical ENTITY state, not a second ledger.

Duplicate and version protection

SOURCE HASH + PROVENANCE + SEMANTIC FINGERPRINT + VERSION RELATIONSHIP + DERIVATIVE RELATIONSHIP + EXISTING-DCO LOOKUP

The factory distinguishes a new DCO from a new version, a derivative and a duplicate instead of multiplying copies of the same underlying asset.

Scarcity classes

ClassPolicy
OPEN_CAPACITYAdditional series may be issued under a published policy.
CONTROLLED_CAPACITYFurther issuance requires a disclosed policy/version.
FIXED_CAPMaximum supply is fixed for the DCO version.
UNIQUEOne unit for a specific exclusive variant such as a territory.

Supply is not price

Factory planning establishes supply, reserve and scarcity. It does not create market value. Pricing remains in offers, RFQs, auctions and settled trades.

10,000-record qualification

The factory test campaign includes a 10,000-Master-Economic-Record registry qualification. That qualifies the catalog/factory path; it does not claim that 10,000 DCOs have already been commercially issued, traded or independently valued.
