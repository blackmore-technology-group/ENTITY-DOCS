# Institutional and Business Briefing

Public discussion draft — 20 October 2026

# How institutions could participate

This publication is an invitation to consider **pilot collaboration and future procurement**, not a claim that a live market has been authorized or that any specific dataset is presently offered for sale.

## Four potential relationships

- **Research buyer:** commission or license an eligible qualified data product with defined authorized use and acceptance criteria.
- **Validation partner:** independently reproduce a technical finding, document negative results and test portability.
- **Research contributor:** provide lawfully controlled datasets, expertise or laboratory services under signed rights arrangements.
- **Infrastructure partner:** support consent management, security, independent audit, lawful payments or secure computation.

## Before procurement

Request a technical specification, permissible use statement, proof of chain-of-rights, data minimization approach, independent qualification evidence where claimed, correction and recall process, retention terms, dispute handling, price structure and audit rights. Where outputs are anonymized or aggregated, require a reasoned assessment of re-identification risk instead of accepting that label at face value.

## Evaluation design

A pilot statement of work should specify the research question, baseline methods, prohibited data, sampling/quality tests, error budget, reproducibility thresholds, compute and privacy costs, responsible reviewers and stopping conditions. Commercial performance should be based on collected payments for delivered services—not hypothetical trading volumes or unsigned letters of interest.

## No institutional endorsement implied

Contact, conversation, demo attendance or procurement review must not be described as approval by an organization, community or government. Any public partnership announcement needs authorization from the parties involved.
