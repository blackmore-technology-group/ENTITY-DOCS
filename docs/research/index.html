Evidence and reproducibility boundary

The released software records BTG-controlled regression, repository-safety, dependency-review, public-conformance and protected-state recovery evidence. Those results are engineering evidence for the identified release; they are not presented as unrelated third-party validation.

Independent evidence is intentionally separated into public reproduction, interoperability, security-audit, hardware, multi-host, certified-device and assessor pathways. A negative independent result is evidence and should not be rewritten into a pass.

A Software Heritage archival request has been submitted for the public repository. This page does not claim a completed public archive identifier until a resolvable Software Heritage record is available. Zenodo metadata is prepared, but no DOI is claimed until Zenodo actually issues one.

For reviewers

When publishing analysis of ENTITY, identify the exact release/tag/commit, the material actually reviewed, test environment, methodology, exclusions and whether the work was independent of BTG. That keeps conclusions bounded to reproducible evidence rather than a generic project name.
