Vector and Memnox were not just patch ingestion

The external-contribution campaign uses real software work outside BTG as evidence-bearing economic events. The important object is not simply “a patch stored in ENTITY”; it is the durable lineage connecting a real problem, contributor, exact change-set, independent review, CI, upstream acceptance, rights context, governed evidence state and any later commercial consequence.

EXTERNAL PROBLEM → CONTRIBUTOR → CHANGE-SET → VALIDATION → UPSTREAM ACCEPTANCE → ENTITY/BTDU LINEAGE → ECONOMIC STATE → OPTIONAL MARKET INSTRUMENT → OPTIONAL SETTLEMENT

Why this is economic lineage

ENTITY’s economic architecture is designed so a contribution does not need an existing bounty or price before its technical history can become economically useful. The accepted event can remain permanently linked to the contributor and evidence; later commercial terms can reference that same history rather than recreating or rewriting it.

The larger market fabric around that lineage

The same ENTITY runtime contains the exchange and recovery machinery for venues, rights-based instruments, balances, listings, disclosures, orders/order books, trades, clearing, entitlements, usage, surveillance, RFQs/quotes/acceptances, cancellations, revenue rules, authorized settlement verifiers and payment attestations. Those capabilities are the economic infrastructure into which a contribution lineage may later participate.

Important evidence boundary: Vector and Memnox prove real external accepted contribution lineage and its current economic state. Their acceptance receipts do not claim that a paid market trade, every exchange table, or external cash settlement occurred. Platform capability and case-specific evidence are intentionally kept separate.

Contribution as an instrument class

The Exchange Protocol explicitly supports a CONTRIBUTION instrument class alongside spot licences, subscriptions, compute-to-data, procurement and secondary licences. That means accepted contribution histories can become a first-class input to economic workflows when an issuer with valid authority creates the relevant instrument and terms.

Causal economic graph

CONTRIBUTOR → SOURCE / CONTRIBUTION → RIGHT / LICENSE → USAGE → DERIVED ASSET / PRODUCT → TRANSACTION → REVENUE → SETTLEMENT

ENTITY’s causal-economic graph requires evidence on causal edges and does not equate correlation with causation. Economic observations are explicitly not accounting fair value, and protocol records do not determine legal entitlement by themselves.
