Portability & Recovery

Layer boundary: this page describes the broader runtime portability/recovery model. Protocol 1.0 independent qualification uses the exact export/recovery requirements in its sealed conformance material.

Portability is the ability to preserve and interpret governed ENTITY state outside the originating provider/runtime context. Recovery restores continuity of an existing identity/state under the applicable recovery rules rather than silently creating a replacement identity and calling it the same Entity.

Continuity conditions

  • Preserve the applicable Entity ID.
  • Verify the required key rotation/recovery chain.
  • Keep historical signatures verifiable.
  • Keep rights/evidence/provenance interpretations bound to their original versions.
  • Avoid making the old hosting/provider environment the sovereign authority required to interpret recovered state.

Strong runtime test

Export from environment A, destroy or isolate the applicable state, recover in environment B, recompute the required deterministic/semantic commitments, and verify that the same qualified authorization/state conclusions hold.

Evidence boundary

A BTG-controlled recovery test is runtime engineering evidence. Full independent Protocol 1.0 recovery/interoperability evidence requires an independently controlled implementation to satisfy the published external gates.
