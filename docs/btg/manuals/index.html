Operating manuals

Public information edition 0.1 · 20 October 2026 · Blackmore Technology Group Limited
Document status: Public explanatory draft. Engineering and legal status must be independently verified before relying on any operating or investment claim. No securities are offered here.

Public operating manuals

Public manuals explain workflows, terminology, safeguards and the responsibilities of each participant. They deliberately exclude source code, administrative credentials and confidential security implementation.

Individual's manual — BTG Bridge

A participating individual should be able to understand which sources are supported, which activity types have been selected, where information is processed, what results may be retained, how to revoke future permissions and whether any proposed commercial release is separately authorized. The full product manual will include tested screenshots only after the interface is qualified.

Researcher's manual — FarmNodes

A researcher should be able to submit an authorized problem definition, select permitted inputs, specify resource and scientific validation limits, reproduce a qualifying result, examine failures and submit assets for independent evidence review. A FarmNode name is not proof of an independent compute host.

Rights holder's manual — ENTITY and Rights Passport

A rights holder should be able to present chain-of-title evidence, describe sublicensing restrictions, review permitted jurisdictions, manage revocation and disputes, and receive auditable statements of licensed economic activity where the agreement provides for them.

Commercial customer's manual

A customer should evaluate dataset coverage, licence type, permitted fields, expiration, reproducibility, customer support and disclosure limits before acquiring a data-rights product. The customer does not automatically receive personal source records or proprietary executable source code.

Prospective investor's manual

A prospective investor must first verify an authorized issuer, legal offering materials, eligibility, risks, instrument rights, transfer restrictions and funds handling. This portal currently provides educational material only; it has no investment enrollment or payment flow.

What is not yet published

Final production screenshots, operational API references, service-level agreements, public fee tariff and externally certified audit reports will not be invented. They will be versioned as the relevant services and approvals become real.
