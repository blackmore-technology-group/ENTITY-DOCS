Partnerships

Test one real economic, authority or data-governance problem.

ENTITY partnerships should exercise the platform where its complete architecture matters: identity, delegated authority, rights, market execution, economic participation, evidence, settlement and recovery. The goal is a bounded external result, not a vague platform endorsement.

High-value pilot tracks

Digital Rights Market Lab

Take a real governed dataset, software contribution, model output or digital asset; define explicit rights; issue an instrument; list it; execute through order-book, auction or RFQ; clear it; create entitlements; record usage; and verify settlement evidence separately.

External Contribution Economy Lab

Use a real external engineering task to test contribution identity, exact change-set lineage, review/acceptance evidence, contribution instruments, service economics and settlement without rewriting upstream licence rights.

Agent Authority & Economic Action Lab

AI agent → persistent identity → delegated authority → permitted purchase/license/execute action → evidence → revocation/recovery. Test whether a model can participate economically without becoming the authority root.

Portable Market Recovery Lab

Export and restore venues, instruments, balances, orders, trades, entitlements, usage, revenue rules and settlement evidence across infrastructure boundaries while preserving semantic hashes and controller attestations.

Sovereign Data Rights Lab

Apply inspect/query/train/derive/redistribute/commercialize rights to real data and observe provenance, usage, derivation and downstream economic consequence.

Independent BTDU Qualification

Researchers control workload, comparison systems, methodology and conclusions for information representation, causal/economic lineage and scaling tests.

Where ENTITY can intersect

Integrate with existing systems instead of replacing them.

Markets & data exchanges

Layer persistent authority, rights, economic participation, settlement evidence and recovery around existing exchanges or data spaces.

Payments & settlement

Keep payment rails external while ENTITY records obligations, authorized verifier evidence and entitlement consequences.

Agentic AI

Bound purchase, licence, access, execution and publication actions to delegated authority rather than model intent alone.

Standards & credentials

Use established credentials, identifiers and policy languages as inputs without claiming ENTITY replaces their issuers or standards bodies.

Institutional recovery

Test whether governed identity, rights and economic state survive provider/infrastructure loss without creating new privilege.

Independent research

Security review, formal methods, market-state recovery, interoperability, reproducibility and BTDU performance qualification.

Current real-world starting point

Vector and Memnox already provide two accepted external contribution lineages.

Both contributions were merged upstream and recorded by ENTITY as UPSTREAM_ACCEPTED_RECORDED. Both remain pro bono with USD 0 realized cash. A partner can therefore start from an existing real-world evidence chain rather than a synthetic example.
