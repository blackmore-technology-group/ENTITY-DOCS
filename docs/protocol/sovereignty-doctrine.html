Sovereign Authority Doctrine

Sovereign authority is authority rooted in the applicable Entity identity/control relationship rather than inferred from infrastructure possession.

Runtime consequences

  • A cloud provider that stores state is not automatically the Entity or its sovereign authority.
  • A DNS/resolver service that helps discovery is not general authority.
  • A device holding a local copy does not automatically gain ownership or delegation.
  • An application acts only under applicable explicit scoped authority.
  • Provider replacement must not by itself destroy identity continuity.
  • Recovery authority is explicit and distinct from ordinary operational/signing authority where the applicable rules make that distinction.

BTG stewardship

Blackmore Technology Group Limited (BTG) stewards the canonical ENTITY project/release lineage and official repositories. That stewardship is intentionally separate from user sovereignty. A conforming deployment is not intended to require BTG hosting, BTG DNS or a mandatory BTG cloud service to make a user's identity authoritative.

Canonical origin versus downstream assets

Canonical ENTITY status preserves ENTITY's own origin lineage. A fork may operate independently; replacing/removing that canonical origin can make it a non-canonical derivative of ENTITY. That classification does not transfer ownership of user or upstream assets to BTG.

Version boundary

ENTITY v3.4.3 is the current supported runtime. The Blackmore Technology Data Universe (BTDU) component remains 3.4.2 unchanged. Historical v3.4.2 origin evidence remains historical evidence; it is not rewritten to appear current.
