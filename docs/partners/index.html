ENTITY v3.4.3 · strategic partnerships

Sovereign digital infrastructure should be tested by institutions, not only by its creator

ENTITY is open infrastructure for persistent, machine-verifiable relationships among identity, authority, information, AI activity, rights, evidence, economic events and recovery. Blackmore Technology Group is seeking organizations that can independently test where that architecture is useful, where it fails, and where it can interoperate with established standards and systems.

Partnership principle: We are not asking organizations to replace their identity, authorization, data-space, credential, AI or market infrastructure. We want to test whether ENTITY can connect those systems while preserving authority, provenance, rights, evidence and recovery boundaries.

Six partnership tracks

Standards & digital trust

Map ENTITY primitives to existing trust frameworks, governance requirements and interoperability standards.

Agentic AI governance

Test whether autonomous systems can act only within explicit delegated authority while retaining a complete evidence and provenance trail.

Sovereign data & data spaces

Carry machine-readable rights, provenance and usage obligations across organizational and provider boundaries.

Organizational trust & credentials

Use established credentials as trusted inputs to persistent authority and portable governed relationships rather than replacing the credential issuer.

Independent research & qualification

Give unrelated researchers control over methodology, workloads and conclusions for protocol and BTDU evaluation.

Economic infrastructure

Test whether digital rights, authorized usage, evidence, obligations and recovery can remain connected through real institutional workflows.

What BTG will provide

  • ENTITY v3.4.3 source and public protocol material.
  • Current conformance and qualification evidence with explicit claim boundaries.
  • Technical implementation support for a bounded pilot.
  • Documented separation between identity, authority, rights, evidence, economic state and AI reasoning.
  • Open-source continuity so a pilot is not dependent on a closed proprietary runtime.

What BTG needs from a serious partner

  • Independent technical or institutional scrutiny.
  • A real problem, workload, integration boundary or test environment.
  • Agreement on what would count as success, failure or inconclusive evidence before testing begins.
  • Clear ownership, publication and confidentiality boundaries.
  • Willingness to distinguish internal engineering evidence from independent validation.
Current claim boundary: ENTITY v3.4.3 is a supported open-source runtime with internal engineering qualification. It is not claimed to be globally adopted infrastructure, independent legal proof, a universal BTDU performance guarantee, or proof that modeled economic relationships have settled in the external world.
