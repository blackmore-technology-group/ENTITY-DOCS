Economic model

Rights Instruments

An economic instrument packages a bounded, machine-verifiable subset of rights around an underlying Digital Commodity Object. The instrument can be offered, negotiated, traded or exercised only within authority already established by the DCO's active Rights Passport.

Core rule: DCO Rights Passport = maximum authorized actions; Economic Instrument = selected subset of those actions. An instrument cannot expand the Rights Passport.

Issuance

The issuer must have authority over the underlying DCO/right. The wallet reads the selected DCO's current Rights Passport and exposes only explicit ALLOW actions as directly selectable instrument rights. Prohibited actions are not selectable; conditional/non-ALLOW actions are not silently treated as grants. The canonical market registry re-validates the selected action subset before instrument creation and fails closed if actions exceed the Rights Passport.

Example

In the qualified 2026-10-03 production state, BIRFR-1 allows BENCHMARK, CERTIFY, COMPUTE, DERIVE, EVALUATE, FINE_TUNE, INFER, OEM_DEPLOY and TRAIN. It does not allow COMMERCIALIZE, so the wallet does not propose COMMERCIALIZE for BIRFR-1. Selecting TRAIN can suggest the issuer-scoped symbol BIRFR1-TRN. Another DCO may legitimately allow COMMERCIALIZE when its own Rights Passport explicitly grants it.

Instrument identity and ticker

A DCO does not receive a ticker merely because it exists. Tickers/market identifiers are attached to explicitly issued instruments and remain subordinate to immutable canonical instrument identity. A DCO with no instrument is shown as NO TICKER.

Listing information

A listing requires a signed, versioned Asset Disclosure for the underlying DCO. The Listing Information Sheet combines that disclosure with canonical DCO, Global Passport, Rights Passport, domain/profile, provenance, evidence and market terms so a buyer can understand what the instrument actually represents.

Secondary transfer and use

Secondary transfer preserves issuance/transfer lineage and only terms validly attached to the instrument. Exercise/usage activates what the instrument actually grants. Usage evidence may later support settlement, derivative lineage or economic consequence, but execution is not itself proof of external payment.
