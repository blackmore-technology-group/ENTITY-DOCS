Common evidence controls

  • No modification of the canonical v3.4.2 release.
  • No access to BTG private signing material is required.
  • Evidence should be reproducible or independently reviewable.
  • Evidence artifacts should be cryptographically hashed.
  • Participants should disclose material relationships with BTG.
  • Passing one gate does not imply passing another.
  • Failures and incomplete results must be preserved rather than rewritten as passes.

Why these calls are public

GitHub provides timestamped discussion history, attributable participants, linked repositories/commits, CI evidence and a public chain of review. The objective is not to create the appearance of validation; it is to recruit unrelated parties who can produce evidence BTG cannot legitimately self-issue.
