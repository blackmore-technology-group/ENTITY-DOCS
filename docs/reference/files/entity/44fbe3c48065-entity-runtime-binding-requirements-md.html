# Canonical Runtime Binding — rights_claims
Authority: `rights_claims`
Canonical owner: `04_Entity_Registry\ownership_graphs`

- Rights SHALL use a many-to-many Rights & Claims Graph, not a single-owner shortcut.
- Claims remain independent of content assets and support claimant, right type, share, scope, territory, jurisdiction, legal basis, evidence, signature, verification and dispute state.
- Registration time or valid signature SHALL NOT automatically resolve ownership/authorship.
- Competing claims MAY coexist and SHALL be surfaced rather than silently collapsed.
- Verified provenance SHALL remain distinct from verified rights.

## READY Gate
- Tests cover multiple claimants, conflicting claims, supersession, dispute, invalid signer and evidence-level distinctions.
