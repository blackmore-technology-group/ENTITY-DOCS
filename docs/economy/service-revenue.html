Commercial services

Service Revenue

An open protocol can support commercial services without requiring a universal protocol tax.

Possible service layers

  • Canonical certification/validation.
  • Managed ENTITY infrastructure.
  • Registries and Global Passport services.
  • Enterprise/government integration.
  • Market data, analytics and indexes.
  • Clearing/settlement or venue services where applicable.
  • Support, training and professional services.
  • BTG-originated DCO/right products.

Commercial boundary

Fees must arise from a service contract, market venue, instrument term or other explicit commercial relationship. The protocol's canonical lineage does not itself charge users.
