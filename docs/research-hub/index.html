BTDU benchmark boundary

Controlled results are hypotheses for outside testing—not universal guarantees.

Published controlled representation result

In one BTG-controlled comparison involving roughly 50 million entities and 250 million bonds, the tested BTDU representation was about 4.4 GB versus about 153.6 GB for the compared 768-dimensional float32 vector representation—about 97.14% lower representation storage in that specific setup.

This compares representation storage only; it does not prove universal gains for every database, model, RAM or compute workload.

Stronger scaling question

Can the architecture sustain hundreds of millions or billions of useful relationships while materially reducing infrastructure costs across realistic workloads selected by independent researchers?

  • researcher controls workload
  • researcher controls comparison
  • methodology published
  • negative results retained
  • storage, RAM, compute and economic claims measured separately

Research collaboration model

Outside researchers control the conclusion.

  • BTG supplies source, public evidence and falsifiable hypotheses
  • research partner selects methodology, workload and comparison systems
  • results may contradict BTG expectations
  • failed reproduction remains publishable evidence
  • feature capability and case-specific evidence remain separately labeled
Studying ENTITY does not make a university, researcher or assessor an endorser. The useful output is independently controlled evidence—positive, negative or mixed.
