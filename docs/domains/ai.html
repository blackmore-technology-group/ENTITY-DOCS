Artificial Intelligence

The AI package applies ENTITY identity, authority, evidence, provenance and rights to AI lifecycle artifacts. v3.4.2's BTDU adds a governed atomic/bonded information substrate beneath that model.

Mappings

NIST AI RMF · SPDX 3 · CycloneDX. The mappings are correspondence layers and do not claim certification or normative equivalence.

Lifecycle uses

Govern training/evaluation datasets, model/software artifacts, evidence, approvals, licences, usage and derivatives while keeping cryptographic validity separate from model quality/safety claims. SPDX/CycloneDX mappings can connect software/supply-chain evidence; AI RMF mapping can add governance context without becoming an AI RMF certification.

BTDU + AI

BTDU can preserve exact objects and information relationships; NIKI is intended to receive bounded projections rather than become sovereign authority. The v3.4.2 release does not claim REAL_WORLD_TRAINING complete; that remains post-release qualification evidence.
