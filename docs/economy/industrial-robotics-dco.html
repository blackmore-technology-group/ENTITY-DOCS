Seven-product core remains intact

The original seven-product commercial core totals 16,410 rights units: inference, training, compute-to-data, commercial derivative, redistribution, ten regional-exclusive units and enterprise/API. The five added strategic archetypes—evaluation, OEM/embedded, field-of-use, synthetic/derived data and contributor participation—bring the reference DCO to 28,418 total heterogeneous rights units.

Field-of-use

The eight field slots cover warehouse automation, manufacturing, mining robotics, agricultural robotics, construction robotics, logistics, inspection/maintenance and research. A field must be bound at contract activation.

OEM / embedded

The initial OEM series contains 500 units with one unit defined as capacity for 100 authorized deployed robots/devices, representing 50,000 devices of initial deployment capacity.

Contributor participation

The contributor series contains 10,000 fixed weighting units. Allocation requires accepted provenance evidence and does not create an automatic cash entitlement; a revenue-pool rule must exist before distribution.

Master Economic Record

The installed DCO now has one Master Economic Record covering identity, version, economic profile, instrument/archetype summary, supply, treasury position, market state, economic state and factory classification.
