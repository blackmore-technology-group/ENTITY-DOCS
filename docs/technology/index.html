Architecture

Technology with explicit trust boundaries.

ENTITY is designed so identity, authority, information, reasoning, rights, evidence, events, value and recovery can interact without collapsing into one undifferentiated trust model.

IDENTITY → AUTHORITY → INFORMATION → INTELLIGENCE → RIGHTS → EVIDENCE → EVENTS → VALUE → RECOVERY → CONTINUITY
Core layers

Persistent identity & delegated authority

ENTITY treats identity and authority as separate concepts. A subject can persist while its permissions change. Authority can be scoped to particular actions, resources, durations and contexts, and can be revoked without silently expanding privilege.

Authority deep dive →

Information & governed state

ENTITY records state transitions with provenance and evidence boundaries. External observations, signatures, attestations and governed conclusions remain distinguishable so verification does not become an automatic claim of objective truth.

Protocol manual →

ADAM deterministic execution

ADAM is the deterministic state execution/transformation layer. It operates on governed state and relationships; it is not an independent authority source and does not create rights by itself.

NIKI bounded intelligence

NIKI provides reasoning and projection over governed information. It is deliberately downstream of authority and evidence. AI output is not automatically permission, ownership, entitlement or external truth.

Rights & provenance

Trade or govern rights—not merely copies.

Data can be copied without destroying the original. ENTITY therefore models the economically meaningful object as the governed right: access, inspect, query, train, derive, redistribute, commercialize, sublicense, research or compute.

DCO → INSTRUMENT → LISTING → DISCLOSURE → ORDER / RFQ / AUCTION → PRICE DISCOVERY → TRADE → CLEARING → SETTLEMENT → ENTITLEMENT → USAGE → DERIVED OUTPUT → ECONOMIC CONSEQUENCE

Provenance

Origin, custody, authorization, transformation, derivation, contribution, evidence, usage and economic events can be recorded as separate facts.

Rights

Rights can be machine-readable and bounded by scope, policy and evidence rather than inferred from storage possession or protocol origin.

Value

Modeled economic relationships do not automatically prove payment, legal settlement or realized value. External evidence remains necessary where the claim depends on the outside world.

BTDU

The Blackmore Technology Data Universe.

BTDU is the governed information substrate behind the current 3.4.2 component: reusable atoms, recursive compounds, bonds, relationships, indexes, lineage and rights-aware state.

Reusable atoms

Letters, numbers and symbols can be represented as reusable base atoms rather than repeatedly storing equivalent primitive structures.

Recursive compounds

Atoms can bond into words, code, mathematical expressions and higher-order structures while retaining governed relationships and provenance.

Controlled benchmark boundary

BTG has recorded large controlled representation-storage reductions in specific benchmark configurations. Those results are not presented as universal superiority or proof that all model/RAM/compute workloads improve equally.

Recovery & continuity

Infrastructure possession is not sovereignty.

ENTITY is intended to preserve governed identity, authority and state across application, provider or infrastructure change, provided the relevant recovery and qualification requirements are satisfied.

  • portable governed state
  • explicit recovery authority
  • provider-independent verification
  • historical interpretation preserved
  • revocation and continuity treated separately
Hosting, custody, registration, mirroring, resolution or possession of infrastructure do not automatically become sovereign authority. Recovery must not silently create privilege that did not exist before failure.
Interoperability

ENTITY should map to established ecosystems rather than redefine them.

Identity & credentials

External credentials may be inputs to an ENTITY authority flow without ENTITY pretending to replace the issuer.

Authorization

Established authorization decisions can be consumed or exposed at a boundary while ENTITY preserves downstream authority, evidence, state and recovery semantics.

Data spaces

ENTITY can operate as a rights/provenance and recovery layer around governed data exchanges instead of claiming to replace complete data-space ecosystems.
