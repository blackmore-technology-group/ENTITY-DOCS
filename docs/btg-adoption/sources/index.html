Documentation sources and research standard

The portal is based primarily on BTG's October 19, 2026 Data Investment Regulatory and Engineering Master Dossier and the public-discussion whitepaper The Knowledge Capital Economy. These internal documents are project specifications and author statements, not independent verification of every claim.

Claims that require additional scrutiny

Estimated industry data-broker revenue, value of national data investment, economic projections, numbers of executable FarmNodes, invention benchmarks, privacy controls and platform readiness should be cited to current, relevant sources and checked against their definitions. An estimate of industry revenue is not the value of datasets transferred in a single year, an investment exchange's volume or BTG revenue.

Publication integrity policy

Published research should show an author or responsible body, publication date, scope, hyperlinks, evidence quality, corrections and known limitations. The portal intentionally avoids made-up case studies, real prices, trading volume, investor returns and regulatory credentials.
