Research

Test the claims that matter.

ENTITY research is structured around falsifiable questions: can unrelated implementers reproduce semantics, can authority/recovery properties be verified, and can BTDU materially reduce representation and infrastructure costs under independently controlled workloads?

Independent implementation

Can an unrelated engineer reproduce ENTITY authority/provenance behavior using only public material?

Formal & security analysis

Do delegation, revocation, recovery and fail-closed boundaries behave as claimed under adversarial conditions?

BTDU performance

Under what workloads do reusable atoms/bonds reduce representation storage, RAM, compute or model requirements—and where do they not?

BTDU benchmark boundary

Controlled results are hypotheses for outside testing—not universal guarantees.

Published controlled representation result

In one BTG-controlled comparison involving roughly 50 million entities and 250 million bonds, the tested BTDU representation was about 4.4 GB versus about 153.6 GB for the compared 768-dimensional float32 vector representation—about 97.14% lower representation storage in that specific setup.

This is a controlled comparison of representation storage, not proof that every vector system, model, database or AI workload will see the same result.

What remains to be independently proven

The stronger Level 3 question is whether the architecture can support hundreds of millions or billions of useful relationships and materially reduce model size, RAM, compute and infrastructure requirements across realistic workloads selected by independent researchers.

  • researcher controls workload
  • researcher controls comparison system
  • methodology published
  • negative results retained
  • economic/compute claims measured separately

Research collaboration model

Outside researchers control the conclusion.

  • BTG supplies source, public evidence and falsifiable hypotheses
  • research partner chooses methodology and comparison systems
  • results can contradict BTG expectations
  • failed reproduction remains publishable evidence
  • BTG-controlled test results remain clearly labeled
A university, researcher or assessor does not become an endorser merely by studying ENTITY. The useful outcome is independently controlled evidence—positive, negative or mixed.
