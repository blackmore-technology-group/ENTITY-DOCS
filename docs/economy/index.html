Economic & Market Fabric

ENTITY is an economic operating fabric built on explicit identity, authority, rights, events and value. Its implementation includes venues, rights-based instruments, balances, listings, disclosures, order books, call auctions, RFQs/quotes, trades, clearing, entitlements, usage, surveillance, cancellations, revenue rules, settlement verifiers, payment attestations, treasury participation and provider-independent market recovery.

Sovereignty is a property of this system—not the whole system. Provider independence protects the continuity of authority, rights, market positions, evidence and economic lineage. The developer-facing object is the complete governed economy.

Rights and productive capital

ENTITY does not require artificial scarcity of information bytes. Scarcity can exist in rights, entitlements, capacity, duration, jurisdiction, usage quantity, derivation, participation and transferability. A dataset can remain copyable while access, training, compute, derivative or commercialization rights remain bounded and economically meaningful.
