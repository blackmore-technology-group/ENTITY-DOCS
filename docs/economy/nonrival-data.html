Economic model

Non-rival Data

Data is economically unusual because giving another party a copy does not necessarily deprive the source holder of the same bytes. ENTITY therefore models scarcity primarily in rights and entitlements rather than pretending every copy is a unique physical object.

Scarce dimensions

  • Who may access or query.
  • What purpose is permitted.
  • Whether AI training or derivation is allowed.
  • Exclusivity or field-of-use.
  • Duration, quantity, capacity or geography.
  • Redistribution or sublicensing.
  • Revenue/derivative participation.

Why this matters

A market for data rights can support repeat licensing, subscriptions, usage-based access and derivatives without requiring artificial byte scarcity or a mandatory protocol token.
