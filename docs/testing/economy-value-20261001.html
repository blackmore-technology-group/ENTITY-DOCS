Accumulation result

The EOPP treasury path finished with 12,362 synthetic CAD-denominated obligations: 10,862 settled-attested and 1,500 accrued. Separately, multi-contributor rights trades produced 16,000 distribution-event units. These are distinct paths; trade gross was not added again to the 28,362 figure.

The treasury also produced a 50,000-unit indicative mark from 200 reserve units × the observed last price of 250. ENTITY explicitly labels that mark indicative_only, not_accounting_fair_value and not_protocol_generated_value.

Why this matters to developers

The test demonstrates a programmable chain from DCO/data object through rights instruments, disclosures, listings, order-book or RFQ execution, clearing/settlement, entitlements, usage, derivative consequences, service receivables, contributor distributions and treasury snapshots. Economic participation occurs only because explicit policy terms were created; provenance alone did not create a royalty or price.
