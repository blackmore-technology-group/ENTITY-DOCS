# Trading rights is not always trading securities

A permission to run software or use an authorized dataset may be governed primarily by commercial contracts and relevant privacy/intellectual-property law. A financial interest offered in expectation of profit may be a security. The actual legal rights and economic structure decide the category.

## Canadian market path under consideration

BTG's October 2026 dossier identifies consultation with securities counsel and the British Columbia Securities Commission as important steps. It distinguishes a potentially exempt **primary issuance** from a later **authorized secondary marketplace**. Exempt offerings have investor eligibility, filing and resale restrictions, while an exchange or alternative trading system has separate marketplace and dealer requirements.

## Disclosure controls

A proposed listing must not represent registration, recognition, approval, guaranteed returns, liquidity or audited value unless that exact status is established. Regulator discussions are not endorsements. BTG's proposed internal tickers and proof tokens are not official securities identifiers unless assigned through the applicable processes.
