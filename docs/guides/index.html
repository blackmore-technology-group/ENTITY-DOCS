Architecture comparisons

ENTITY vs IAM

Conventional IAM controls accounts and access inside administrative domains. ENTITY adds portable authority, provenance, rights, governed transitions and sovereign recovery.

Compare ENTITY and IAM →

ENTITY vs verifiable credentials

Signed credentials prove issuer attribution and integrity; ENTITY separately models authority, rights, events, evidence and recovery.

Compare the trust layers →

ENTITY vs blockchain provenance

Ledgers provide ordering and replicated history; ENTITY focuses on authority, rights, provenance boundaries, recovery and explicit economic consequence.
