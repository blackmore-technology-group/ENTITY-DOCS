Blackmore Technology Data Universe

Atomic and bonded data under explicit authority and provenance

BTDU represents reusable atoms, bonds, compounds and governed objects while ENTITY supplies authority, rights, provenance and economic semantics.

Architecture boundary

BTDU is not a replacement name for ENTITY and it is not a generic compression claim. ENTITY remains the authority, rights and economic layer. ADAM is the deterministic atom/bond state engine. BTDU adds governed topology and indexes. NIKI may reason over bounded projections without becoming sovereign authority.

Atoms → Bonds → Compounds → Governed Objects → Provenance / Rights / Economic Lineage

What can be represented

  • reusable symbolic atoms and compounds;
  • exact-byte compounds for reconstruction;
  • repository manifests and aggregate roots;
  • source, controller and rights-holder relationships;
  • evidence and provenance edges;
  • economic nodes and rights-aware relationships;
  • temporary computational relationships versus relationships that require persistent evidentiary or economic meaning.

What topology does not do

A graph edge or bond does not silently create ownership, authority or economic entitlement. Those states require the appropriate ENTITY authority and rights semantics.

Why developers may care

The architecture is intended to let structured knowledge reuse common atomic components rather than duplicating every semantic relationship as an isolated model parameter or opaque vector entry. The relevant question is not whether BTDU replaces every storage system; it is whether a governed atomic/bonded representation can reduce semantic and indexing overhead for applicable workloads while preserving lineage and reconstruction.

Challenge the implementation

The released implementation is public. Reviewers are explicitly invited to find counterexamples involving authorization, reconstruction, provenance separation, portability, performance or economic-boundary mistakes.
