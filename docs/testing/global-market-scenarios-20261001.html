Global market scenario proof

One DCO was exercised across four distinct venue/currency configurations with repeated primary licensing, secondary resale, derivative participation, contributor distributions, settlement attestations and VALUE-state records.

What this proves: ENTITY can preserve and accumulate separate economic records for one data object across controlled CA/CAD, US/USD, EU/EUR and JP/JPY market scenarios. What it does not prove: real-world demand, real cash revenue, FX conversion, participant domicile, legal enforceability, liquidity or fair market value.

Secondary and derivative accumulation

Secondary resale created originator royalties in all four currencies. Three derivative-revenue rounds per market then accumulated additional participation, with the first two rounds settlement-attested and the third left accrued to prove state separation.

Cross-market scenario

A scenario actor labelled “EU Primary Buyer” acquired rights on the US venue in USD through the supported order-book path: 5 units × 220 = 1,100 USD gross, creating a 220-unit EOPP primary obligation. The actor label is scenario metadata, not evidence of legal domicile.

VALUE primitive

ENTITY recorded separate SETTLED and ACCRUED VALUE records for each currency using the EOPP synthetic obligations as the basis. Every VALUE record retains asserted_value_is_not_market_value=true.

RFQ boundary discovered

Known integration gap: EEP RFQ creation, quote acceptance and settlement succeeded, but EOPP cannot yet capture that RFQ trade because the resulting trade has no deterministic sell_order_id for seller-signature re-verification. This is tracked as ENTITY issue #117. The proof does not silently count that RFQ toward EOPP accumulation.

Integrity controls

Duplicate derivative occurrence, wrong currency, oversell, unauthorized settlement and tampered signed order were all rejected fail-closed.
