Investor information

This portal is not a prospectus, offering memorandum, solicitation, investment recommendation or invitation to deposit money. No live BTG investment subscription or secondary trading service is offered here.

The economic distinctions

A commercial licence typically grants specified use of data or executable software. A royalty or revenue entitlement may be contractual. A security can represent equity, debt, a profit-sharing arrangement or another regulated investment interest. The security classification depends on law and economic substance, not BTG's chosen product label.

Principal risks

  • Regulatory: an exemption for issuing securities is not permission to operate a trading venue.
  • Commercial: buyers may not materialize, product demand can change and cash flows may be zero.
  • Rights: provenance may be contested, third-party licences may restrict use, or privacy issues may require withdrawal.
  • Valuation: historic investment, hypothetical market size and proposed prices do not establish an asset's realizable value.
  • Liquidity: investors might have no permitted or practical resale venue.
  • Technical: systems can fail, security controls can be bypassed and scientific outputs can be incorrect.
  • Conflicts: platform, issuer, contributors and investors may have different interests.

Controls required before any offering

Verified issuer and legal rights; reviewed offering terms; suitable corporate records; qualified legal advice on securities classification, exemptions, dealer and marketplace obligations; investor eligibility where applicable; reconciled banking; truthful disclosures; cyber/privacy assurance; and a board-authorized operational release.

Current position

The source dossier dated October 19, 2026 states that its described investment package had not been filed and no regulated exchange status had been authorized by that package. The public status must be reverified before any future launch announcement.
