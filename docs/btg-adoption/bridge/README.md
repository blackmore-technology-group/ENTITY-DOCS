# BTG Bridge guide

Public information edition 0.1 | 20 October 2026

# A bridge for authorized digital activity

**BTG Bridge** is the proposed consumer-facing entry point to the data economy. Its purpose is to help an individual decide which permitted activity, if any, can be processed through a personal FarmNode. It is separate from BTG Exchange.

## What an individual could contribute

Potential supported sources include web-search activity, permitted browser history, selected social-media activity, public posts, product comparisons and other deliberately connected sources. Coverage depends on operating-system permissions, provider APIs, account-export rules and contracts. No single app can universally inspect all other apps.

## Intended user workflow

1. Review supported source types and plain-language notices before connecting anything.
2. Choose exactly which categories to contribute; exclude medical, banking, identity, credentials and other restricted categories by default.
3. Review which purposes are allowed: private analysis, research participation or an expressly authorized commercial contribution.
4. Approve each permission and see a record of what the Bridge collected or derived.
5. Revoke future use where possible, export an activity report and submit deletion/access requests subject to applicable lawful retention.
6. Separately approve any proposed market release. Connecting a platform must **never** automatically list personal activity for sale.

## Important limitations

A conversation can contain another person's personal information. Recording and commercialization require an appropriate lawful basis for all affected parties; a participant's consent may not be sufficient by itself. Social-media or search exports may be restricted by the original platform's contract, even if downloaded by the account holder. Pseudonymized data can still identify someone when linked to other records.

**No surveillance promise:** This documentation does not claim that BTG Bridge can intercept private apps, record calls silently, bypass provider restrictions or guarantee earnings.

## Individual rights

The intended design includes a readable consent receipt, purpose limitation, contribution history, access controls, withdrawal procedures, rights-dispute handling and transparent compensation agreements. Those rights are product requirements; deployed controls must be demonstrated before accepting live participant data.
