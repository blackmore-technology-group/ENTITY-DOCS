Manufacturing

The Manufacturing package configures ENTITY for governed industrial assets and data while preserving source/authority/provenance across systems.

Mappings

OPC UA · Asset Administration Shell. The mappings do not replace those external specifications.

Required deployment facts

  • organization
  • jurisdiction
  • authority_source
  • asset_namespace

Useful model

An industrial asset can have an ENTITY identity or governed relationship, evidence about observations/maintenance, rights over telemetry or designs, provenance through transformations, and portable state independent of the vendor hosting the operational platform.

BTDU relevance

BTDU can represent large numbers of asset/data relationships and exact evidence objects while ENTITY retains authority and rights semantics.
