BTG Public Economic Briefing

Public discussion draft · 20 October 2026 · Blackmore Technology Group Limited
Document status: Explanatory adoption draft. No investment offer or claim of regulated market authorization.

Why a more participatory data economy matters

Daily digital activities create useful information. Businesses invest in collecting and analyzing it; the value comes from a combination of original activity, processing, expertise, legal permission and customer demand. The individual whose activity contributed may lack transparency or direct compensation. BTG proposes to address that gap by creating pathways for voluntary, controlled participation, not by claiming everyone automatically owns every data fact.

The proposed BTG model

  1. A participant makes a specific choice about whether to connect an authorized activity source to BTG Bridge.
  2. Permitted inputs can be processed privately by Personal FarmNodes to produce research signals and derived outputs.
  3. NIKI, BTDU and reusable DataChips can support research and qualification. Their capabilities require task-specific testing; unverified inventions must not be presented as proven.
  4. ENTITY records provenance, permissions and evidence. A signed record is not itself legal proof that an asset is owned or tradable.
  5. Where permitted, qualified research products may be supplied under contractual rights. Investment securities require separate legal structures and approvals.

Benefits BTG will investigate

  • More meaningful decisions for individuals about commercial reuse of eligible digital activity.
  • Opportunity for consent-based contributor income where validated customer demand and enforceable agreements exist.
  • Accessible, independently assessable research products for local businesses and institutions.
  • New commercialization models for small research teams, including groups outside technology centres.

What this does not promise

There is no guaranteed price for someone's searches, posts or browsing. Participation will not make private conversations public. The proposed marketplace is not represented here as a regulated, operational securities exchange. No citizen or public institution is being asked to endorse, invest in or surrender data through this document.

Public engagement commitment

Publish the specific uses, evidence, limitations, safeguards, fee assumptions and contributor arrangements before recruitment. Hear from privacy specialists, First Nations and communities, individual participants, prospective customers, lawyers and independent researchers. Document criticisms alongside proposed responses.

Current publication status

Concept and adoption plan, 20 October 2026. Technical implementations and legal authorizations must be checked against current evidence before any public deployment claims.
