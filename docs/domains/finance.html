Finance

The Finance package applies ENTITY identity, authority, evidence, rights and portable passport state to financial-system integration without redefining financial standards.

Mappings

ISO 20022 · FIX · LEI. ENTITY mappings describe correspondence and version context. External standards/registries remain authoritative for their own semantics.

Required deployment facts

  • organization
  • jurisdiction
  • authority_source
  • settlement_policy

Use with market lifecycle

Finance deployments can bind participant authority, rights/instruments, orders/trades, clearing/settlement evidence and entitlements to persistent provenance. The protocol must still distinguish a valid record from legal enforceability and accounting treatment.
