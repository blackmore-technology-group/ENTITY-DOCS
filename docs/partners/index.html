ENTITY v3.4.3 · strategic partnerships

Test the digital-economic fabric against a real institutional problem

ENTITY connects identity, authority, rights, governed information, market execution, economic participation, settlement evidence and recovery. Blackmore Technology Group is seeking organizations that can independently test where that architecture creates value, where it fails and how it can interoperate with existing market, data, credential, payment and AI systems.

Partnership principle: ENTITY does not need to replace a bank, exchange, data space, identity provider, credential issuer, cloud platform or payment rail. A useful pilot tests whether ENTITY can preserve rights, authority, provenance, economic state and recovery across those existing systems.

Partnership tracks

Digital rights markets

Issue and exchange explicit rights around datasets, software, models, compute, research or other governed digital assets through venues, listings, orders, RFQs or auctions.

Contribution economy

Connect accepted external technical work to durable contributor/change/evidence/economic lineage and later valid commercial instruments without rewriting upstream licence rights.

Settlement & payments

Integrate external payment rails while preserving obligations, authorized verifier evidence, entitlement changes and the distinction between execution and settlement.

Agentic AI economic authority

Test AI agents that may purchase, license, access, execute or publish only within explicit delegated authority and recoverable evidence boundaries.

Sovereign data & data spaces

Carry machine-readable rights, provenance, usage, derivation and economic participation across organizational and provider boundaries.

Market-state recovery

Recover venues, instruments, balances, orders, trades, entitlements, usage, revenue rules, treasuries and settlement evidence from portable logical state.

Independent research & security

Give outside researchers control over methodology, workloads, attack models, comparisons and conclusions.

Standards & interoperability

Map ENTITY primitives to established credential, rights, provenance, finance and data-space ecosystems without claiming false equivalence or endorsement.

Real external evidence already available

Vector #26501 / PR #26504 and Memnox #46 / PR #86 are upstream-merged, ENTITY-recorded external contribution cases. Both are pro bono with USD 0 realized cash, providing a clean existing baseline for studying contribution lineage without conflating technical acceptance with payment.

What a serious pilot should define first

  • the real object or workflow being governed;
  • who has authority and which rights are valid;
  • which market/economic stages are in scope;
  • what external evidence is required for settlement or truth claims;
  • what failure, recovery and negative cases will be tested;
  • what result the partner controls and may publish independently.
Current claim boundary: ENTITY is not presented as globally adopted market infrastructure, legal title by itself, an automatic royalty system, or proof that every modeled value is realized cash. The platform implements the economic machinery; external use and settlement must be evidenced separately.
