# Canonical Runtime Binding — portable_state
Authority: `portable_state`
Canonical owner: `15_Operations\backups`

- Owner-controlled export SHALL include enough documented state to recover/interpret identity, assets, claims, policies, contracts, provenance, ledger, usage and economic records.
- No proprietary database SHALL be the sole authoritative interpretation format.
- Backups SHALL be encrypted and restore-tested; a successful backup without demonstrated restore is insufficient.
- Portable encrypted content MAY be included when requested/authorized.
- Loss of BTG infrastructure SHALL NOT make sovereign records unrecoverable or uninterpretable.

## READY Gate
- Qualification performs fresh backup and restore of identity, asset metadata, rights graph, policies, ledger, contracts and economic records.
- Restored signatures/evidence remain independently verifiable.
